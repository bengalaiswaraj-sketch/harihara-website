Section I • Sacred Philosophy

Two Divine Forms. One Eternal Essence.

Just as inhaling and exhaling are two actions of a single life-breath, Vishnu (Hari) and Shiva (Hara) are two expressions of the one Supreme Consciousness.

❖ ॐ ❖
Left Half of the Portal • Sri Hari

Lord Vishnu — The All-Pervading Preserver

Radiant royal-blue complexion, crowned with the golden Kirit Mukut, adorned in yellow Pitambara silk, bearing the Sudarshana Chakra, Panchajanya Shankha, and Kaustubha gem — embodying cosmic order, compassion, and sustenance.

  • Hari (हरि): He who uplifts the soul and removes sorrow, adharma, and cosmic disharmony.
  • Vishnu (विष्णु): From root viṣ — the All-Pervading Consciousness present in every atom.
  • Cosmic Role: Sthiti — Sustaining creation with love, Dharma, and protective grace.
हरिहर Ekattvam
Right Half of the Portal • Mahadeva

Lord Shiva — The Auspicious Transformer

Ash-blue meditative radiance, crowned with matted locks holding the Ganga and crescent moon, adorned with Tripundra, Rudraksha, and Vasuki, wielding the Trishula and Damaru — embodying supreme stillness, renewal, and liberation.

  • Hara (हर): He who dissolves ignorance, ego, and the bonds of worldly illusion.
  • Shiva (शिव): The Auspicious, Pure, Unchanging Witness Consciousness (Chit).
  • Cosmic Role: Samhara & Anugraha — Clearing the old for new creation and granting Moksha.
